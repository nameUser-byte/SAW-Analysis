My contribution

Requested row-by-row comparison of supplied and recalculated OOC labels and checked calculation ranges and formulas, while a teammate reviewed the reference rules.

Actions and deliverables

Documented the criterion discrepancies and separated original team work from the later AI-assisted validation. In the historical full-data proxy comparison, 1,128 of 1,365 supplied OOC rows were not reproduced by a simple 3-sigma check. This is not proof of false alarms.

What the evidence supports

A later conditional benchmark used 4,000 evaluation rows, including 273 OOC rows. Logistic ROC-AUC was 0.5107; useful future OOC prediction was not established. Missing sensors and label-generation mechanisms remain hypotheses requiring further evidence.

Educational synthetic data. The official subgroup structure and OOC rules are unknown. Non-significant tests do not establish absence of an effect or independence.
